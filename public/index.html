How it works

Four steps, all running live.

  1. 01

    Index your clips

    Connect Twitch and Callback pulls your clip library through Helix, years of it, then embeds each title, game and date into a private vector index.

  2. 02

    Listen to the room

    A rolling window of live chat is watched for velocity spikes, phrases repeated by many different chatters, raids and category changes.

  3. 03

    Find the rhyme

    The window is embedded and scored against every clip with hybrid retrieval: meaning, shared keywords, game, and how far the best clip leads the next one.

  4. 04

    Play it back

    Above the confidence line and outside cooldown, the overlay rewinds to the old clip and the bot posts the link so chat can watch it again.

Restraint is the feature

It stays quiet until it's sure.

A wrong callback on stream is worse than none. Every candidate is scored on four signals, and it has to clear your threshold, a cooldown, a no-repeat rule and a short veto window before anything reaches the stream.

  • Cooldown between callbacks, tunable from 30 seconds to 30 minutes
  • Three-second veto window in the control panel before each fire
  • Only clips the streamer indexed from their own channel

OBS setup

One browser source. Thirty seconds.

  1. Copy your overlay link from the control panel once your clips are indexed.
  2. Add a Browser source in OBS, paste the link, set 1920 × 1080.
  3. Keep the control panel open while you stream. Press "Send test" to check it lines up.
